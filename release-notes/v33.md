DQIX Enhanced v33: Dynamic Widescreen battles and fixes from your reports.

**What's new since v32**

Dynamic Widescreen
- **Battle submenus show again**: spells, abilities, items and changing
  equipment kept the party's panels but lost their lists and descriptions;
  now the commands screen shows whole while they are open.
- **The party's panels keep their order** while you choose each one's
  actions (the one choosing no longer jumps to the top).
- **Fighting alone** shows your panel in the bottom left too, instead of
  the small screen in the corner, a panel's height higher so it's easy to
  see, and it stays there when you open spells, abilities or items.
- **A long battle message** (Examine) that can't fit beside the panels
  goes above them instead of under them.
- **Dialogue boxes stay in the middle** when their name tag and Yes/No sit
  on them together (the Krak Pot, Dr Phlegming).
- **The Krak Pot no longer swaps the screens** while you pick ingredients:
  the pot and the item's description stay big, the recipe list small in
  the corner. Stella's Alchemimicon still shows its list big.
- **The turnstile in Gortress's prison** no longer jumps to another place
  with a long thin beam across the floor (widescreen drew a part of it from
  too far away).

Thanks to everyone who sent bug reports, here and on GitHub.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`. **Already on v32?** The game
offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves
and settings are kept.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v33.

The plans for what comes next are in ROADMAP.md.
