DQIX Enhanced v34.1: a fix for RecompLauncher.

**What's new since v34**

- **"Failed loading SDL3 library" in RecompLauncher**: fixed. The game
  itself uses SDL2, but RecompLauncher also looks for SDL3.dll next to the
  game; the build now puts it there. (If you added it by hand, nothing to
  do: it's replaced by the same file.)
- **DQIX Enhanced is on RecompLauncher**:
  https://recomplauncher.com/game/dqix-enhanced. Choose your ROM, and it
  builds, starts and updates the game for you.

Thanks to Mingo for the report and Flardarmar for finding the fix, and to
NelsonMai for Transmog in v34.

**How to install**: through RecompLauncher, or download *Source code (zip)*
below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat` (Linux:
`./BUILD.sh` with your ROM). **Already on v34 or v33?** The game offers the
update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and
settings are kept.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v34.1.
